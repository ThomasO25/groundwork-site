# Client Intake Questionnaire

Use this to scope, price, and kick off a new website/automation project in one pass.
It is ordered so the answers flow naturally in a discovery call, and it maps onto the
site's quote-form fields (name, business, email, phone, industry, budget, message,
lead source) so web leads and manually-onboarded clients produce the same data.

**How to use it:** send as a form (Google Form / Typeform / Formspree), or work
through it live on a call and record answers in the `leads`/`projects` tables from
`docs/DASHBOARD_PLAN.md`. Fields marked **(required to quote)** are the minimum needed
to give a real number; the rest sharpen scope and design.

---

## Section 1 — Business basics

1. **(required to quote)** Business name, and the legal entity name if different.
2. **(required to quote)** Primary contact: name, role, best phone, best email.
3. **(required to quote)** What does the business do, in one sentence?
4. **(required to quote)** Industry / trade (e.g. plumbing, HVAC, dental, salon, legal,
   restaurant, landscaping, auto).
5. Years in business, and roughly how many people work there.
6. Service area — the towns/zip codes/radius you actually serve. (Drives local SEO.)
7. Business address. Is it a storefront customers visit, or service-only / mobile?
8. Hours of operation, including any seasonal changes.

## Section 2 — Goals & success

9. **(required to quote)** What's the #1 thing this website must do?
   (e.g. more phone calls, more quote requests, online booking, look credible,
   replace an outdated site, rank on Google.)
10. How do customers reach you today — phone, text, form, walk-in, referral, social?
11. Roughly how many new leads/customers per month now, and what would "this worked"
    look like in 90 days? (A concrete number helps us design toward it.)
12. What's the average value of a job/customer? (Helps justify scope and ROI.)
13. Which action matters most on the site: **call**, **request a quote**, or **book an
    appointment**? (We make that the primary button everywhere.)

## Section 3 — Current situation

14. Do you have a website now? If so, the URL, and what's wrong with it.
15. Who owns your **domain name**, and do you know the login? (Common blocker at launch.)
16. Do you have a business email on your domain (e.g. you@yourbusiness.com)?
17. Do you have a **Google Business Profile**? Is it claimed/verified? Link if yes.
18. Are you running any ads now (Google, Facebook, etc.)? Where does that traffic go?
19. Any existing tools we should connect to — CRM, scheduling, invoicing, POS,
    email marketing, phone/text system?

## Section 4 — Content & brand assets

*(Anything not ready yet is fine — we'll use clearly-labeled placeholders and swap in
real assets before launch. We never publish invented reviews, results, or credentials.)*

20. **Logo** — do you have one (vector/PNG preferred)? If not, do you need one?
21. **Brand colors / fonts** — any existing brand guide, or preferences/dislikes?
22. **Photos** — do you have real photos of your team, work, storefront, products?
    (Real photos dramatically outperform stock for local trust.)
23. **Services list** — the exact services you want listed, in priority order.
24. **Pricing** — should any prices show on the site, or route everything to a quote?
25. **Reviews/testimonials** — where do your reviews live (Google, Facebook, Yelp)?
    Can we display real ones with permission? (We will not fabricate any.)
26. **Credentials** — licenses, insurance, certifications, associations, warranties,
    guarantees you want featured. (Only real, verifiable ones.)
27. **Awards / press / "as seen in"** — only if genuine and you can point to the source.

## Section 5 — Functionality & scope

28. **(required to quote)** Which features do you need? (Check all that apply.)
    - [ ] Contact / quote request form
    - [ ] Click-to-call + click-to-text
    - [ ] Online booking / appointment scheduling
    - [ ] Service-area pages / multi-location
    - [ ] Photo gallery / before-&-after
    - [ ] Reviews/testimonials display
    - [ ] Blog / articles
    - [ ] Live chat / chatbot
    - [ ] Payments / deposits / invoicing
    - [ ] Automated follow-ups (email/text after a lead comes in)
    - [ ] CRM / lead pipeline
    - [ ] Other: __________
29. Roughly how many pages do you expect? (Home + services + about + contact is typical
    for a starter.)
30. Do you need to edit content yourself after launch, or should we handle updates?
31. Any languages beyond English?
32. Any accessibility, legal, or compliance requirements specific to your industry?

## Section 6 — Timeline & budget

33. **(required to quote)** Target launch date, and anything driving it (season, event,
    ad campaign, expiring current site).
34. **(required to quote)** Budget range for the initial build:
    - [ ] Under $2,000  - [ ] $2,000–$3,500  - [ ] $3,500–$7,500
    - [ ] $7,500+  - [ ] Not sure — advise me
35. Are you open to a **monthly care plan** (hosting, updates, security, small changes,
    support)? This keeps the site healthy and is how most of our clients operate.
36. Who is the decision-maker, and who else needs to approve the design/copy?

## Section 7 — Style & references

37. Two or three websites you like (competitors or not) and what you like about each.
38. Any sites you dislike — and why.
39. Words that should describe how your business feels to a customer
    (e.g. trustworthy, fast, premium, friendly, no-nonsense, local, established).
40. Anything else we should know before we start?
